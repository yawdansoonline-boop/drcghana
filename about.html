The Story Behind DRCGHANA

DRCGHANA is the digital design arm of Del Rhema Construction — bringing professional, ready-to-build architectural and structural designs to Ghana, online.

Company Introduction

Who We Are

DRCGHANA is the digital design arm of Del Rhema Construction, a proudly Ghanaian-owned company duly registered with the Registrar General of Ghana. Del Rhema Construction specializes in General Building Construction, Civil Engineering, Plumbing and Electrical Engineering, and Architectural and Structural Designing — bringing together the skills, resources, and partnerships needed to deliver projects our clients can be proud of.

Through DRCGHANA, we've made our professional design expertise available online — giving landowners, contractors, and homeowners across Ghana instant access to ready-to-build architectural and structural design packages, without the wait of a full custom commission.
